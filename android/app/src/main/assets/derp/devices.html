Your devices

  1. On your computer, open Derp’s Settings, then You and your dork.
  2. Under Your devices, choose Pair a phone.
  3. Point this phone’s camera at the code, and open the link in Derp.

Your bookmarks, passwords and open tabs are encrypted on your devices, with a key only they have. Derp’s relay passes them along without being able to read them, and there’s still no account.
