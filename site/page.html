Why Derp

Everything you like about Firefox, minus the clutter.

Derp runs Mozilla’s official engine, so websites just work. The rest is rebuilt to feel calm, quick and a bit more fun.

Fluid

Spring-physics scrolling with momentum, elastic overscroll and small motions everywhere. Reduced-motion settings are respected.

Fast

GPU-accelerated drawing, tuned networking, and uBlock Origin already installed, so pages load without the junk.

Your dork

Derp’s account is a cartoon you design. It lives on your computer: no sign-in, no email, no servers.

Private by default

No telemetry, studies, sponsored tiles or Mozilla services. AI features start switched off; turn on only what you want.

Web apps

Install any site as an app with its own window and icon. On Linux and Windows.

Memory Saver

Tabs you haven’t touched in a while take a nap and wake up when you click them. Pinned and playing tabs stay awake.

JPEG XL

The sharper, smaller image format is switched on, so JPEG XL images just show up.

Updates itself

Derp and its engine update in the background, checked against their signatures, and swap in the next time you open it.

Firefox inside

On computers the installer gets Firefox straight from Mozilla and checks Mozilla’s signature. On Android, Derp runs GeckoView, Mozilla’s engine for apps.

  • Telemetry
  • Studies and experiments
  • Sponsored tiles and stories
  • Mozilla account and Sync
  • Crash reports to Mozilla
  • AI you didn’t ask for

Your dork

No account. Just you, as a cartoon.

Derp doesn’t do sign-ins. You get a dork instead: a little cartoon you design, with a name. Pick a face, eyes, mouth, hair or a hat, glasses, headphones and a backdrop.

Your dork sits at the top of the window and on Derp’s settings page. It’s stored only on your computer and never sent anywhere.

Psst. The little Derp in the corner likes being poked.

Install

One command. About a minute.

Paste this into a terminal. Derp installs into your user account, so you don’t need admin rights.

In a terminal:

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/{{REPO}}/main/install.sh | bash
  • Works onx86-64 and ARM64. Needs curl, gpg and Python 3.9 or newer.
  • ThenDerp opens and has you make your dork. Later, open it from your app grid or run derp.
  • Make it defaultin your system settings, or run xdg-settings set default-web-browser derp.desktop.

In Terminal:

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/{{REPO}}/main/install.sh | bash
  • Works onApple silicon and Intel Macs.
  • ThenDerp opens and has you make your dork. It lives in your Applications folder.
  • Make it defaultin System Settings → Desktop & Dock → Default web browser.

In PowerShell:

> irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/{{REPO}}/main/install.ps1 | iex
  • Works onWindows 10 and 11, x64 and ARM64.
  • ThenDerp opens and has you make your dork. Later, find it in the Start menu.
  • Make it defaultin Settings → Apps → Default apps → Derp.

On your phone or tablet:

Download Derp for Android
  • Works onAndroid 8 or newer. That download fits nearly every phone; for older 32-bit phones get ARM, for Chromebooks x86-64.
  • Thenopen the download. Android asks to let your browser install apps once; allow it. Derp opens and has you make your dork.
  • UpdatesDerp checks for new versions itself and offers them. It’s built on GeckoView, Mozilla’s Firefox engine for apps.

Prefer to look first? Download the latest release and run its installer, or read how it works and how to uninstall.
